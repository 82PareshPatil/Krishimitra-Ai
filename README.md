# 🌾 KrishiMitra AI – Empowering Farmers with AI

🔗 **Live Demo Video:**  
[![Watch on YouTube](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/t0Bds-y_o7c/hqdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/t0Bds-y_o7c?si=Rt6j7gweF2Oylh9k)

> 🚜 KrishiMitra AI is a smart agriculture platform built to solve real-world problems faced by farmers and consumers using **Artificial Intelligence**, **community support**, and **direct farm-to-consumer marketplace**.

---

## 📖 Abstract

KrishiMitra AI improves the lives of farmers by providing a digital platform where they can:

- Reduce costs on seeds, fertilizers, and equipment via **community buying & rental**
- Detect plant diseases early with **AI tools**
- Sell their crops directly to consumers, hotels, and online platforms — eliminating middlemen
- Receive guidance from expert farmers and collaborate in a digital farming community

Consumers get **fresh**, **organic**, and **affordable produce** directly from the farms.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement

Farmers often suffer from:

- High fertilizer/tool prices
- No expert guidance
- Middlemen cutting into profits
- Lack of storage or digital marketplace access

Consumers, on the other hand, pay high prices and can't verify food quality.

---

## ✅ Solution

KrishiMitra AI bridges this gap with:

- 🌱 **Farmer Community Platform**  
- 🧑‍🏫 **Expert Q&A & Chat Support**  
- 🛠️ **Tool & Equipment Rental**  
- 📦 **Smart Storage Integration**  
- 🧠 **AI-Based Disease Detection & Crop Advice**  
- 🛒 **Direct Selling Marketplace**  
- 📊 **Digital Invoicing & Inventory**  
- 📱 **Mobile & Web Interface (Ongoing)**

---

## 🔍 System Overview

### 🔄 Workflow:
1. Farmer connects to community  
2. Gets crop & disease insights via AI  
3. Rents tools or buys collectively  
4. Stores produce  
5. Sells directly to consumers or vendors  
6. Track everything via dashboard  

### 🧱 Architecture:
- **Frontend:** React.js, Bootstrap  
- **Backend:** Flask (AI), PHP  
- **Database:** MySQL  
- **Security:** Hashing  
- **AI Models:** Python, ML, CNN  
- **Hosting:** Vercel, AWS  
- **Payment:** Razorpay
